This observation connects to Emily Esfahani Smith's argument that care and support are often built through small everyday actions. Smith explains that strong relationships grow through "kindness and generosity" rather than dramatic gestures. This classroom scene supports her idea because the support here is through teaching and attention. The teacher was patient, and the students paid attention, which made the class a good place to learn. Even small actions like explaining examples clearly or listening carefully can help students feel more supported and connected in college classrooms.
