This observation connects to Arthur Brooks's argument that love helps reduce fear and isolation. Brooks explains that people often feel less anxious when they experience connection and support from others. He writes that "perfect love drives out fear" (Brooks). Brooks also argues that loneliness and lack of social connection can make people feel more afraid and isolated. This photo supports his idea because the students are creating a welcoming environment through simple conversations and by taking part together. Even small interactions on campus can help students feel more connected to the community around them.
